# Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and protect private information shared during support or security work.

我们致力于维护尊重、包容且免受骚扰的社区。请保持建设性沟通，讨论方案而非攻击个人，并保护支持与安全处理过程中接触到的隐私信息。

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, publishing another person's private information, or sustained disruption. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

For a private conduct report, contact the maintainers through a private GitHub Security Advisory and state that the report concerns community conduct. Maintainers will review it confidentially and avoid conflicts of interest where possible.
